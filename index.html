Addressing the Rural Agricultural Challenge

Field assessment findings in Dakamarri Village, Bheemunipatnam Mandal.

⏳

Information Gap

Farmers frequently travel 8 to 15 km toward Tagarapuvalasa and Anandapuram without knowing if critical agricultural inputs are stocked, risking lost days during peak sowing periods.

⚖️

Price Disparity

Without centralized price visibility, input prices for hybrid seeds and crop protection chemicals vary between outlets, placing extra financial burden on smallholder households.

📱

AgriLocal Platform

A lightweight, dual-language directory indexing certified village vendors, product availability, benchmark prices, and verified direct contact details optimized for low-bandwidth networks.
